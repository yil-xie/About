History of Food Park Tambayan



Food Park Tambayan began as a small campus food stall with a simple goal: make great food accessible and affordable for students. Over time it grew into a lively food park where local vendors share flavors, events bring the community together, and convenient services like delivery and reservations were added. Clean As You Go (CLAYGO) Reminder

📢 Attention Students of Davao del Sur State College! 🌿

Our school is strongly implementing the CLAYGO (Clean As You Go) policy and promoting sustainable practices, such as the ban on single-use plastics. As responsible students, it is our duty to uphold these rules and contribute to a cleaner, greener campus. 🌏✨

👉 If you eat at the Food Park or anywhere on campus, please dispose of your garbage properly and always leave the place cleaner than you found it. Remember, small actions lead to big changes — and together, we can build a culture of discipline, respect, and sustainability. 💚

2024

Grand Launching

The DSSC Food Park officially opened in 2024, bringing together local vendors and students for its first big launch event. It became an instant hit for its variety of food choices and relaxing campus vibe.

Now

Continuing the Journey

The Food Park continues to grow, welcoming new stalls, improving services, and offering a space where students and locals can enjoy fresh, affordable meals every day.
